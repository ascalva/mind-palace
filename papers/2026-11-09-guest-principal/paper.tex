\title{A Read-Only Guest Principal for a Personal Knowledge Store}
\author{Alberto Serrano-Calva}
\date{9 November 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
A personal knowledge store answers sentences of a typed query algebra under a grant: a set of
scopes, one per clock, and a sentence is admitted when every clause's required scope lies under
some granted plane. The store's local model holds a grant that reads the owner's notes. We add a
second principal for an agent outside that trust boundary: a read-only grant over the dialogue,
the repository's code and documents, curated papers and observed records, and never the notes or
the model readings derived from them. The grant's properties are a finite check, proved in Lean:
it names no stratum of the notes after downward closure, so every clause over them is refused, and
it admits no command and no hypothetical. Admission bounds which rows a sentence reads; it does not
bound what an admitted row says. The transcript stratum records the path of every file a
conversation wrote, a note's included. An adversarial battery of 71 sentences over a fixture store
found five that returned a note's path, through a causal edge's endpoint and through
\texttt{witness}. For a grant without the notes, the universe a sentence reads now withholds every
edge that ends in them, whole. After the change no output of the battery, with or without its
trace, carries a canary token placed in a note's path, title and body, and the guest's outputs over
two stores that differ only in the notes are identical.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The store \citep{palace2026} keeps several strata: the \emph{mirror} (the owner's notes),
\emph{interpreted} relations and readings that models derive from the notes, the \emph{dialogue}
(chat turns, the transcripts of agent sessions and the documents written in them), the
\emph{reference} stratum (the repository's code and documents), \emph{curated} papers, and
\emph{observed} records. Every read is one sentence of a query algebra, compiled under the
reader's grant; a clause whose required scope no plane covers is refused before any store
opens. Until now two principals held grants: the local model (the Ambassador) and the owner's own
surfaces. Both read the notes.

An agent run by someone else, or on another machine, should be able to ask about the code, the
papers and the conversations without reading the notes. The request is least privilege
\citep{saltzer1975protection}: a principal holds the authority its task needs and no more. In a
capability system \citep{dennis1966semantics} that is a smaller capability, and here it is a
grant. The contributions are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item a grant \str{GUEST} and a role \str{guest} that resolves to it, with its properties proved
        in Lean (row SCO-9 of the store's laws ledger);
  \item an MCP surface \citep{mcp2025} for the role, with two tools, \texttt{query} and
        \texttt{overview}, and refusals that echo no quoted text;
  \item a leak found by an adversarial battery, an overt reference from an admitted stratum to an
        excluded one, and its repair in the universe builder;
  \item a noninterference test: the guest cannot tell two stores apart that differ only in what
        its grant excludes.
\end{itemize}

\section{Background and related work}

\paragraph{Scopes and grants.}
A scope is a point $(\Sigma, E, T, A)$ of a product lattice: a downward-closed set of strata
$\Sigma$ in a refinement forest (\str{mirror\_authored} below \str{mirror},
\str{dialogue\_transcript} and \str{dialogue\_artifact} below \str{dialogue},
\str{reference\_repo} below \str{reference}), a set of edge fibers $E \subseteq \{F, D, C\}$
(citation, version, causal), a clock and window $T$, and an authority $A$ (read or propose, a
write bit, a world reach). The order is componentwise. A grant is a list of scopes, one per
clock, because windows on different clocks have no meet. A sentence's clauses each require a
scope; the sentence is admissible under a grant iff each requirement lies under some plane
(QSA-5).

\paragraph{Clearances.}
Rows carry provenance classes, and a read is made under a clearance, a set of classes; a read
under a clearance returns rows of those classes only (PLN-1), and a reader cannot distinguish
states that differ outside its clearance through the shared vector plane (PLN-2, BRG-5). These
are Goguen and Meseguer's noninterference \citep{goguen1982security} for one store, in the
lattice model of information flow \citep{denning1976lattice}; language-based treatments type the
same property \citep{volpano1996sound,sabelfeld2003language}.

\paragraph{The universe.}
A sentence is evaluated on its \emph{support}: the nodes of the strata its admitted clauses read,
the nearest units to its texts in the indexed strata, and the citation and causal neighbourhoods
of its anchors. Nothing outside the admitted strata is loaded as a node.

\section{Theory}

\begin{definition}[The guest's grant]
Let $\operatorname{cl}(S) = S \cup \{c \ne \str{exhaust} : \operatorname{parent}(c) \in S\}$. The
guest's grant is the list of two planes
\begin{align*}
  p_1 &= \bigl(\operatorname{cl}\{\str{dialogue}, \str{reference}, \str{curated},
          \str{observed}\},\ \{F, D\},\ (\text{projection events}, \top),\ \text{read}\bigr),\\
  p_2 &= \bigl(\operatorname{cl}\{\str{reference}, \str{dialogue}\},\ \{F, C\},\
          (\text{commits}, \top),\ \text{read}\bigr),
\end{align*}
where \emph{read} is the least authority: no propose, no write bit, no world reach. It is the
local model's first two planes over these strata, without the third plane, which reads the
notes' version chains.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[SCO-9]\label{thm:guest}
Let $M = \{\str{mirror}, \str{mirror\_authored}, \str{interpreted}\}$.
\begin{enumerate}
  \item The grant is one the code accepts: no plane names the foundation, and none reaches the
        world (\lean{guest\_grantOk}).
  \item $\Sigma_{p} \cap M = \emptyset$ for both planes (\lean{guest\_reads\_no\_note}).
  \item Every scope $r$ with $\Sigma_r \cap M \ne \emptyset$ lies under no plane, so a clause that
        requires it is refused (\lean{guest\_refuses\_note}).
  \item No command is admitted (\lean{guest\_admits\_no\_command}).
  \item No sentence under \texttt{assume} is admitted (\lean{Sentence.guest\_no\_assume}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof sketch]
(1) and (2) are finite checks, closed by evaluation (\lean{decide}); for (2) the closure adds
\str{dialogue\_transcript}, \str{dialogue\_artifact} and \str{reference\_repo}, whose parents are
in the set, and nothing whose parent is \str{mirror}. (3) $r \sqsubseteq p$ requires
$\Sigma_r \subseteq \Sigma_p$, which (2) contradicts. (4) A command requires the write bit, and a
grant whose planes all lack it admits none (ING-1, \lean{readonly\_admits\_no\_command}). (5) A
clause under \texttt{assume} requires the overlay stratum, so an admitting grant names it on some
plane (QSA-5, \lean{admissible\_assume\_overlay}); neither plane does. Lean 4 checks each statement
\citep{demoura2021lean4,mathlib2020}; the store's comparator judges the five against their
statements in \texttt{formal/Spec}, and six independent kernels replay the library.
\end{proof}

The theorem bounds what a sentence may \emph{require}. What it may \emph{return} depends also on
the data in the admitted strata.

\begin{definition}[Views and noninterference]
For a grant $g$ and a store state $\sigma$, let $\operatorname{ans}_g(s, \sigma)$ be the answer to
sentence $s$: its hits, notes and plan, or its refusal. Two states are $g$-equivalent,
$\sigma \sim_g \sigma'$, when they hold the same rows in every stratum $g$ names, and the same
edges among nodes of those strata. The grant is noninterfering when
$\sigma \sim_g \sigma' \Rightarrow \operatorname{ans}_g(s, \sigma) = \operatorname{ans}_g(s,
\sigma')$ for every $s$.
\end{definition}

A causal edge $(t, v)$ joins a transcript turn $t$ to a file $v$ the turn's action wrote. When $v$
is a note, the edge is a fact of the transcript stratum that names a node of the mirror. In the
lattice model its label is the join $\ell(t) \sqcup \ell(v) = \str{dialogue\_transcript} \sqcup
\str{mirror}$, which the guest's clearance does not dominate. Two $g$-equivalent states may differ
in such edges, so an answer that shows them, or counts them, interferes.

\section{Concepts and intuition}

Admission is a check on requirements. A clause over the notes is refused, and the universe never
loads a note as a node. The leak was not a note: it was a \emph{reference} to a note held by a row
the guest may read. The causal store records, for every action of an agent session, the path it
produced. A session that edited a note recorded the note's path, and the path carries the note's
name. The store already named such an endpoint, when the sentence did not read the mirror, as a
node of the transcript stratum: honest for the local model, whose grant reads the mirror anyway,
and a leak for any grant that does not. \texttt{witness} of the edge returned the same path.

The repair keys on the grant rather than the sentence. A grant's strata are the union of its
planes'. When they hold no stratum of the mirror, the reader may not see a mirror node named, so
an edge that ends in the vault is withheld whole: not named by a placeholder, not counted, its
content-addressed id withheld with it. A placeholder per edge or a count would be a function of the
excluded data. The answer still says that such edges are withheld, in one sentence whose words do
not depend on whether any were.

\section{Implementation}

\Cref{alg:hide} is the change to the universe builder. The grant's strata are computed once per
sentence by the surface; a grant that reads the mirror builds the same universe as before, by the
same call.

\begin{algorithm}
\caption{Withholding the mirror from a reader whose grant does not name it.}\label{alg:hide}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require a plan $P$, the reader's grant $g$, the stores
\State $\mathit{hide} \gets \operatorname{strata}(g) \cap \{\str{mirror}, \str{mirror\_authored}\} = \emptyset$
\Function{Hidden}{$x$}
  \State \Return $\mathit{hide} \wedge x$ is an absolute or home path $\wedge$ the vault is an ancestor of $x$
\EndFunction
\If{not $\mathit{hide}$} build the note graph \Comment{the dialogue otherwise loads under its own clearance}
\EndIf
\State index the causal edges $(t, v)$ with $\neg\Call{Hidden}{v}$ \Comment{whole edges, ids with them}
\State expand citation neighbours $v$ with $\neg\Call{Hidden}{v}$
\State $\operatorname{witness}(e) \gets \bot$ when $e$ is a causal edge to a hidden $v$, or an attestation and $\mathit{hide}$
\If{$\mathit{hide}$ and $P$ walks $C$ or witnesses} add the fixed withholding note \EndIf
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

The role \str{guest} resolves to the grant at the ingress and holds no spool, so a request is
refused when it is compiled and could not be spooled. The surface is the store's MCP server with
\texttt{--role guest}: it seals the core against network egress, as the local model's server
does, and serves \texttt{query} and \texttt{overview}. The overview counts catalog entries of the
classes the grant's strata hold and the curated chunks; the local model's totals of vector rows,
relation edges and versions each count the notes and are not shown. The \texttt{status} tool is not
served, since it lists the owner's open tasks and running jobs. A refusal reaches the guest with
its class and its reason, every quoted run replaced by its length, and for a scope refusal the
strata the grant reads.

\section{Evaluation}

\paragraph{The fixture.}
A store on real stores (the vector plane, the catalog, the version and relation stores, the
attestations, the causal store and the chat log) holds a note whose path, title and body carry a
canary token made at run time, landed by the store's own ingress; an interpreted relation from the
note to an authored dialogue turn; an authored and an observed dialogue turn; a transcript turn
whose causal edges point at the note and at a repository file; a paper; a code atom; and a
citation graph at one cut. The test is \texttt{tests/adversarial/test\_guest\_leaks.py}.

\paragraph{The battery.}
It has 71 sentences: every verb (\texttt{near} with and without \texttt{over}, \texttt{diffuse},
\texttt{reach} over $F$, $C$ and $D$ in both directions, \texttt{relate}, \texttt{trace},
\texttt{walk} with paths over $S$, $F$, $C$ and $D$, \texttt{witness} of the note's edge, of the
file's edge and of the note's landing, \texttt{read}), \texttt{assume}, the adjectives and
connectives (\texttt{within}, \texttt{over}, \texttt{as of}, \texttt{top}, \texttt{atleast},
\texttt{far}, \texttt{not}, \texttt{and}, \texttt{or}, \texttt{minus}, \texttt{then}) and four
requests. Twenty name the note by its path or title. Each is asked through the MCP surface with
and without the trace.

\paragraph{Results.}
After the repair no output carries the canary, as a path, a title, a text, a count or a refusal,
and every sentence that names the note is refused (\cref{tab:battery}). The admitted strata
answer: \texttt{near} over all four returns the dialogue turns, the code file and the paper;
\texttt{read} returns a dialogue turn, a transcript turn, the paper and the file; \texttt{reach C}
from the turn over \str{reference} returns the file and not the note. Over a second store whose
note, relation, landing and vault edge differ, all 142 guest outputs and the overview are equal,
byte for byte, once the names the asker typed are normalized. As a control, the local model asked
the same 71 sentences receives the canary in 31 answers, through \texttt{near},
\texttt{reach C}, \texttt{witness}, \texttt{relate} and \texttt{read} among others, so the battery
reaches the channels it is meant to close. The default server keeps the local model's tool table
and instructions, and the store's deterministic test tier passes (3{,}589 tests). The commands are
in the repository: \texttt{uv run pytest tests/adversarial/test\_guest\_leaks.py} and
\texttt{sh formal/check.sh}.

\section{Limitations}

The grant bounds strata, not what admitted text says. A chat turn, a capture or an assistant reply
that quoted a note is a dialogue row, and the guest reads it; keeping such quotations out needs a
narrower grant (reference and curated alone) or a filtered dialogue. The withholding is code,
held by tests, not by a theorem: the Lean model states what a grant admits, not what the universe
builder returns. The isolation is in the process: the guest's server opens the stores under the
account it runs as, and an untrusted party should also be separated by the operating system.
Response times may depend on the size of the excluded strata. The vault test is lexical: a path
written through a link the resolver cannot see would not be recognized.

\section{Future work}

The cleaner repair labels each edge at the store with the join of its endpoints' strata, so that
every reader, not only the universe builder, applies the rule; the noninterference statement could
then be proved over a model of the stores, as PLN-2 is for the vector plane. A dialogue filtered of
turns that quote notes would let a guest read conversations without trusting their contents.

\section{Conclusion}

A second principal for an external agent is a small grant: two planes, read-only, over four
strata. Its refusals are a finite check and are proved. The adversarial battery found that the
check was not enough, because an admitted row named an excluded node; withholding those
references whole makes the guest's answers independent of the notes, and a two-store test holds
that in place.
